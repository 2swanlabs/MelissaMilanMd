Our Ethos

Integrating Functional &
Regenerative Medicine

Healthy lifestyle choices that incorporate mindful nutrition, movement, community, and a positive outlook all help to minimize illness and create sustainable, life-long health and wellness.

Dr. Melissa Milan combines Functional Medicine philosophies of treating the root cause of disease with her training in Regenerative Medicine. A competitive athlete — runner, cyclist, triathlete, mountain biker, and rock climber — she believes deeply in the importance of building healthy muscle through weight training.

For those facing more serious illness, using cutting-edge, evidence-based research, Dr. Milan creates a prescriptive lifestyle plan to optimize the management of chronic disease.
